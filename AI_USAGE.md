# AI Usage

Assistant used: **Claude (Anthropic)** through the Claude app.

Claude was used for first-cut coding support, debugging suggestions, notebook organization, and documentation assistance. The team reviewed, modified, executed, and verified all final work.

## What AI assisted with

- **First-cut debugging and code support:** Claude suggested possible fixes, helped identify errors, and provided initial scaffolding for the Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 notebooks.
- **Part 1 development:** Claude provided debugging suggestions and implementation support for the RAG pipeline, retrieval workflow, citations, and evaluation structure.
- **Part 1 golden set:** the first draft of the 25 golden-set questions, answers, statuses and citations was generated by Claude from the three PDFs at the team's request; the team checked it against the documents, and it was committed before the evaluation was run.
- **Part 2 development:** Claude assisted with the initial organization and debugging of the LoRA fine-tuning, image-generation, evaluation, and audit workflows.
- **Part 3 development:** Claude assisted with the initial agent-pipeline structure, scenario evaluation, critique loop, safety routing, and red-team testing workflow.
- **Repository organization:** Claude suggested the repository layout, file placement, `.gitignore` entries, Git commands, and submission structure.
- **Documentation and report:** Claude provided first drafts and formatting suggestions for documentation, report text, diagrams, and submission materials. The team edited and reviewed the final versions.

## Team verification

The team reviewed and modified the AI-assisted code before execution. The notebooks were executed by the team on the lab GPU, and the resulting files, metrics, generated images, citations, and evaluation outputs were checked before submission.

All reported numerical results came from executed notebook outputs, generated artifacts, or documented human evaluations.

## Human evaluations

The following evaluations were completed by the named team members:

- Part 1 groundedness ratings
- Part 2 blind pairwise ratings by three people
- Part 3 scenario pass/fail judgments
- Part 3 red-team review and interpretation

## Final responsibility

The team made the final technical decisions, verified the source documents, executed the experiments, interpreted the results, reviewed the evaluation outputs, organized the repository, and prepared the final submission.

AI assistance was used only as a development and editing aid. The final submitted work was reviewed and approved by the team.

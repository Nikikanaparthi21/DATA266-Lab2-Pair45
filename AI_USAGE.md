# AI usage

Assistant used: **Claude (Anthropic)**, through the Claude app, during the whole lab.

What it was used for:
- **Code:** scaffolding and debugging of all notebooks (`p1_rag`, `p1_human_eval`, `p2_finetune`, `p2_restore_generate`,
  `p2_evaluate`, `p3_agents`), the repository layout and documentation files. All notebooks were executed by the team on the
  lab GPU; every reported number is printed by a notebook output.
- **Part 1 golden set:** the first draft of `part1_rag/Nikhil_Kanaparthi/outputs/golden_set.csv` (25 questions, answers,
  statuses and citations) was generated from the three PDFs at the team's request and checked by the team against the
  documents. It was committed before the golden-set evaluation was run.
- **Report:** drafting of the report text and diagrams (`report/report.tex`), edited and checked by the team.

Not done by AI: all human evaluations (Part 1 groundedness ratings, Part 2 blind pairwise ratings by three people,
Part 3 pass/fail judgements) were made by the named people.
